### Question 1
Which phase of the FinOps lifecycle focuses on rate optimization, rightsizing workloads, and eliminating idle cloud resources?
- [ ] A) Inform
- [x] B) Optimize
- [ ] C) Operate
- [ ] D) Audit

*Explanation: The Optimize phase focuses on identifying cost savings through rate optimization and workload rightsizing.*

### Question 2
What is the primary objective of the FOCUS (FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification) project?
- [ ] A) To replace Kubernetes with virtual machines
- [x] B) To standardize cloud billing data schemas across AWS, Azure, GCP, and SaaS vendors
- [ ] C) To automate Terraform deployment pipelines
- [ ] D) To eliminate public cloud egress charges completely

*Explanation: FOCUS establishes an open standard schema for multi-cloud cost and usage billing data.*
