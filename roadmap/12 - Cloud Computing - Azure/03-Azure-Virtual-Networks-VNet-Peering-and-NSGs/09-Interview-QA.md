# 09 - Interview Q&A: Azure Networking

### Q: What is the priority ordering rule for Network Security Group (NSG) rules?
**Answer:** Rules are processed in numerical order of priority from 100 to 4096. Lower numbers have higher priority and take precedence over higher numbers.
