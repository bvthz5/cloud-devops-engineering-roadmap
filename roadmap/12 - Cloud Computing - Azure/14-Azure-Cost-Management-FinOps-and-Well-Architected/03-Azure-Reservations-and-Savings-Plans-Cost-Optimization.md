# 03 - Azure Reservations & Savings Plans

- **Azure Reservations**: 1 or 3-year commitment on specific VM sizes for up to 72% savings.
- **Azure Savings Plans for Compute**: Flexible commitment across VM series, Azure Functions, and App Services.
