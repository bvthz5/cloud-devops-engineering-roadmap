# 03 - Blob Access Tiers

- **Hot Tier**: Optimized for frequently accessed data.
- **Cool Tier**: Optimized for data stored for at least 30 days.
- **Cold Tier**: Optimized for data stored for at least 90 days.
- **Archive Tier**: Offline storage for at least 180 days (Requires rehydration: hours).
