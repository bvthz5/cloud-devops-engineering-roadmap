# 02 - Resource Groups Architecture & Locks

A Resource Group is a logical container for resources that share the same lifecycle, deployment, and management boundaries.

- **CanNotDelete Lock**: Prevents deletion of resources.
- **ReadOnly Lock**: Prevents modification or deletion of resources.
