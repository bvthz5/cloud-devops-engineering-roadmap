# 09 - Interview Q&A: Galaxy & Collections

### Q: What is an FQCN and why is it recommended?
**Answer:** Fully Qualified Collection Name (e.g., `ansible.builtin.copy`). It prevents namespace collisions when multiple collections implement modules with identical names.
