# 09 - Interview Q&A: AWX & AAP

### Q: What is an Execution Environment (EE) in AAP 2.x?
**Answer:** An EE is a standardized container image containing `ansible-core`, Python dependencies, and collection plugins, ensuring identical playbook execution across environments.
