# 11 - Multiple-Choice Assessment (MCQ)

### Question 1
What is the default pod management policy for a StatefulSet?
- [ ] A) Parallel
- [x] B) OrderedReady
- [ ] C) OneByOne
- [ ] D) Synchronous

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>
`OrderedReady` is the default policy. It ensures pods are deployed, scaled, or upgraded in strict sequential order based on their ordinal index.
</details>
