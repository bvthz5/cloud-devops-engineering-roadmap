---

### Q1. How many usable host IP addresses are available in a `/28` subnet in an AWS VPC?
- [ ] A) 16
- [ ] B) 14
- [ ] C) 11
- [ ] D) 8

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer</b></summary>
<b>Correct Answer: C</b><br>
A /28 subnet has $2^4 = 16$ total IPs. In AWS, 5 IPs are reserved per subnet ($16 - 5 = 11$).
</details>
