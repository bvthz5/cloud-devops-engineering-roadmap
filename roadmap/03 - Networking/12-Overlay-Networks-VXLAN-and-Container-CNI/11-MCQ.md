# 11 - Overlay Networks & CNI: Self-Assessment MCQs

### Q1. What is the standard IANA UDP destination port for VXLAN overlay encapsulation?
- A) 1194
- B) 4789
- C) 51820
- D) 8080
<details><summary><b>View Answer</b></summary><b>Correct Answer: B</b><br>RFC 7348 designates UDP port 4789 as the standard VXLAN destination port.</details>

### Q2. How many unique virtual networks can be represented by a 24-bit VXLAN Network Identifier (VNI)?
- A) 4,096
- B) 65,536
- C) 16,777,216
- D) 4,294,967,296
<details><summary><b>View Answer</b></summary><b>Correct Answer: C</b><br>A 24-bit field allows $2^{24} = 16,777,216$ unique VNIs.</details>
