# 12 — Quick Revision Cheat Sheet: DNS & DHCP

---

## 1. Quick Reference

- **DHCP DORA:** Discover, Offer, Request, Acknowledge (UDP 67/68)
- **A Record:** Domain -> IPv4
- **AAAA Record:** Domain -> IPv6
- **CNAME:** Alias (Subdomains only)
- **ALIAS / ANAME:** Apex alias supported
- **PTR:** Reverse DNS (IP -> Domain)
