# 02 - EC2 Pricing Models: On-Demand, Spot & Savings Plans

- **On-Demand**: Pay per second, no commitment, highest flexibility.
- **Spot Instances**: Up to 90% discount on spare AWS capacity (can be interrupted with 2-minute warning).
- **Savings Plans / Reserved Instances**: 1 or 3-year commitment for up to 72% discount.
