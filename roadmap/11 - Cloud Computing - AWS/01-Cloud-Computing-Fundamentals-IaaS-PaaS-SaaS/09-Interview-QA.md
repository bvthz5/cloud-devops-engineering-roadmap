# 09 - Interview Q&A: Cloud Fundamentals

### Q: Under the AWS Shared Responsibility Model, who is responsible for patching an EC2 operating system?
**Answer:** The customer is responsible for configuring, updating, and patching the guest operating system on EC2 instances.
