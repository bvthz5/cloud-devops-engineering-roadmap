# 03 - Compute Cost Optimization

- **Compute Savings Plans**: Flexible discount up to 66% across EC2, Fargate, and Lambda.
- **Spot Instances**: Up to 90% discount for fault-tolerant workloads.
- **Right-Sizing**: Downsizing over-provisioned EC2 instance sizes based on CPU/RAM utilization.
