# 11 — Multiple Choice Questions: Backup & Archiving

---

### Q1. What does the rsync `-a` flag encapsulate?
- [ ] A) Audio files only
- [ ] B) Archive mode: recurses directories and preserves symlinks, permissions, modification times, owner, and group
- [ ] C) Automatic background execution
- [ ] D) Atomic transaction logging

<details>
<summary><b>View Answer</b></summary>
<b>Correct Answer: B</b><br>
<code>-a</code> (archive) combines <code>-rlptgoD</code> to preserve the exact filesystem attributes.
</details>
