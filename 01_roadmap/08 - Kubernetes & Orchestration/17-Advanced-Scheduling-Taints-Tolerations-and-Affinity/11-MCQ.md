# 11 - Multiple-Choice Assessment (MCQ)

### Question 1
Which taint effect immediately evicts running pods if they do not have a matching toleration?
- [ ] A) NoSchedule
- [ ] B) PreferNoSchedule
- [x] C) NoExecute
- [ ] D) Terminate

<details>
<summary>Explanation</summary>
`NoExecute` evicts any running pods immediately unless they possess a toleration for that taint.
</details>
